03 / THE METHOD

A simple structure, not a magic formula.

The best prompts give an AI enough direction to be genuinely helpful.

01

Give it a role

Describe the expertise and working style you want the assistant to apply. A role is guidance, not a guarantee of expertise.

02

Explain the task

Say what you need, who it is for, and what successful output would contain. Include your constraints and relevant context.

03

Review and refine

Check factual claims, test the result against your objective, and ask follow-up questions. Human judgement remains essential.

04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions worth asking.

What is an AI prompt?

A prompt is the instruction and context you give an AI assistant. It may be a simple question or a more structured brief describing a role, task, constraints and desired output.

Does this work with ChatGPT, Copilot and Claude?

Yes. The builder creates plain-text instructions that you can copy into many text-based AI assistants. Different models may interpret them differently, so review the result.

Does Mightora send my prompt to an AI service?

No. The prompt is assembled locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to an AI model by this website. You choose whether and where to paste it afterwards.

Can I reuse my own AI profiles?

Yes. Edit a role and save it as a custom profile. Custom profiles are stored locally in that browser, not in a cloud account, and may disappear if browser data is cleared.

Is the tool free and available offline?

Yes. There is no account or subscription. You can also download the self-contained offline HTML edition or offline ZIP.
